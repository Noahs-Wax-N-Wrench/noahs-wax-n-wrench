Interior

Interior Detail

Starting at $100

Final price varies by vehicle size and condition.

  • Thorough vacuuming of all interior surfaces
  • Steam cleaning of carpets and upholstery as needed
  • Vinyl, plastic and trim cleaning including dash, console, doors, cupholders, vents and glove box
  • Light spot/stain treatment when agreed upon
  • UV protectant applied to vinyl, plastic and trim
  • Interior glass cleaning
  • Everyday odor elimination

Exterior

BRONZE

Exterior Bronze

Starting at $60+

Degreaser foam and brush cleaning for wheels, wheel wells, tires and lower rocker panels. Pressure washer pre-rinse, foam cannon wash with wash mitt, no-rinse treatment to remove water spots and clean door jambs, finished with spray wax on exterior surfaces.

SILVER

Exterior Silver

Starting at $75+

Everything included with Bronze, plus interior and exterior window cleaning, bug removal treatment, exterior trim and plastic dressing/protectant, and safe tire dressing.

PLATINUM

Exterior Platinum

Starting at $100+

Everything included with Silver and Bronze, plus engine bay cleaning, clay bar decontamination and iron removal treatment.
