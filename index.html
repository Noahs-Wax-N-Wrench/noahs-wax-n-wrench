Our Detailing Packages

Choose the service that fits your vehicle. Final pricing varies depending on vehicle size and condition.

Interior

Interior Detail – Starting at $100

Note: Final price varies by vehicle size and condition.

  • Thorough vacuuming of all interior surfaces
  • Steam cleaning of carpets and upholstery (as needed)
  • Vinyl, plastic & trim cleaning including dash, center console, doors, cupholders, vents, and glove box
  • Light spot/stain treatment (must be agreed upon)
  • UV protectant applied to all vinyl, plastic & trim
  • Interior glass cleaning
  • Everyday odor elimination

Add-ons available: Extractor vacuuming, cabin & or engine air filter replacement, carpet enzyme clean, shampoo & finisher, heavy stain treatment, major odor elimination & more — priced upon consultation.

Exterior

Exterior Bronze – Starting at $60+

Degreaser foam sprayer and brush clean for wheels, wheel wells, tires, and lower rocker panels. Pressure washer pre-rinse, foam cannon agitation wash with wash mitt, no-rinse application to remove any water spots and clean door jambs, finished with a spray wax on all exterior surfaces.

Exterior Silver – Starting at $75+

Everything in Bronze plus: Interior & exterior window cleaning, bug removal treatment, exterior trim & plastic dressing & protectorant, safe tire dressing application.

Exterior Platinum – Starting at $100+

Everything in Silver and Bronze plus: Engine bay cleaning, clay bar decontamination treatment, and iron removal treatment.

💡 Add-ons available for any package (e.g., oil change, headlight restoration, exterior trim restoration, light scratch removal, etc.)
