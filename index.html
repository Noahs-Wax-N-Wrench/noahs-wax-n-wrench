Interior

MOST REQUESTED 01

Interior Detail

Starting at $100

A thorough interior reset designed to bring the cabin back to life while focusing on the areas you see and touch every day.

  • Thorough vacuuming of all interior surfaces
  • Steam cleaning of carpets and upholstery as needed
  • Vinyl, plastic and trim cleaning including dash, console, doors, cupholders, vents and glove box
  • Light spot/stain treatment when agreed upon
  • UV protectant applied to vinyl, plastic and trim
  • Interior glass cleaning
  • Everyday odor elimination
Add-ons available Extractor vacuuming, cabin or engine air filter replacement, carpet enzyme cleaning, shampoo and finisher, heavy stain treatment, major odor elimination and more.
